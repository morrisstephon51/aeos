# Forming Paws — Project Home

**Forming Paws** is a nonprofit platform for safe, healthy, documented dog breeding — geolocation-based matchmaking ("Tinder for dogs"), health-first and community-driven.

## The mission, in one paragraph

Connect dog owners locally so healthy, health-documented dogs can be matched for breeding. Matching and chat unlock only after baseline vet documentation is verified. As revenue grows: (1) a vet-referral pathway to bring under-documented dogs up to health standards, and (2) eventually a physical safe space for mating, breeding, and whelping.

## Core product pillars

1. **Profiles** — owner + dog profiles, photo galleries, structured breed list
2. **Health vault** — document uploads, verification workflow, matching locked until verified
3. **Geo matching** — breed/sex/distance/age filters, mutual-interest unlocks chat
4. **Education hub** — dog health and responsible-breeding content
5. **Safety** — coarse location only, report/block, neutral meetup guidance, anti-puppy-mill rules (litter caps, mandatory docs)

## Business structure

- 501(c)(3) nonprofit; fiscal sponsorship to accept donations before IRS approval
- $0-capital start: free-tier stack (Next.js + Vercel + Supabase), grants, crowdfunding, local vet sponsorships
- Nonprofit-compatible revenue: donations, memberships, verified-badge fees, vet-referral partnerships, sponsored education, future facility fees

# Template — First-Touch Email

Rules: under 120 words. First line = their specific observation, not our intro. One CTA. No attachments. Plain text tone — this must read like a person, because it is one.

---

## Variant A — No Website / Social-Only

**Subject:** `[Business Name]` — customers can't find you

> Hi `[Owner First Name]`,
>
> I was looking for `[niche, e.g., "a barber"]` in `[area]` and found `[Business Name]` on Google — `[specific observation, e.g., "great reviews, but there's no website link, just the Facebook page"]`.
>
> That means people searching "`[niche] near me`" are landing on competitors who do have a site.
>
> I build simple, fast websites for local businesses here in `[city]` — done in about a week, and I handle all the updates after so you never touch it.
>
> Worth a 10-minute call this week? I can show you exactly what it'd look like.
>
> `[Signature — Morris, Plug AI, phone]`
> *(If this isn't useful, reply "no thanks" and I won't follow up.)*

## Variant B — Broken / Outdated Website

**Subject:** noticed something on `[Business Name]`'s website

> Hi `[Owner First Name]`,
>
> I came across `[Business Name]` — `[specific observation, e.g., "your site's menu link is broken on mobile, which is where most of your customers are looking"]`.
>
> You're clearly busy running the business, which is exactly why sites end up like this. I rebuild and then *maintain* websites for local businesses, so it stays current without you thinking about it.
>
> Want me to send over a quick before/after mockup of your homepage? Free, no strings — takes me an hour and you'll see the difference immediately.
>
> `[Signature]`
> *(Reply "no thanks" and I won't follow up.)*

## Personalization Checklist (before marking draft ready)

- [ ] Specific observation is verified true today
- [ ] Owner's actual first name (or "Hi there" — never a wrong guess)
- [ ] Niche phrasing matches how *they* describe themselves
- [ ] Under 120 words, one CTA, opt-out line present

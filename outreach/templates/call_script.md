# Template — Discovery Call Script

Goal of the call: understand their business, agree on scope, and leave with a **yes to receiving a proposal** (not a yes to buying — that's the proposal's job).

---

## Opener (cold call, HOT leads)

> "Hi, is this `[Owner Name]`? This is Morris with Plug AI — I'm a local web guy here in `[city]`, not a call center, I promise. Quick reason I'm calling: `[specific observation]`. Do you have 90 seconds or did I catch you mid-rush?"

If mid-rush: *"Totally get it — when's a better 10 minutes, today or tomorrow?"* Get a time. End.

## Discovery Questions (booked call — pick 4–5, listen more than talk)

1. "How do most of your customers find you right now?"
2. "When someone Googles you, what do you *want* them to see?"
3. "What's the #1 thing you wish customers knew before they walked in / called?"
4. "Have you had a website before? What happened with it?"
5. "Who updates your hours/menu/prices when they change — and how long does that take today?"  ← *maintenance seed*
6. "If the site brought you 2–3 extra customers a week, what's that worth to you?"

## Pitch (only after discovery — mirror their words)

> "Based on what you said, here's what I'd do: `[scope in their language — 'a simple site where people see the menu, your hours, and can call you in one tap']`. That's my `[package name]` at `[price range]`, live in `[timeframe]`. And the part most people skip: I stay on it after launch — `[maintenance plan]` at `[price]`/month means when your `[hours/menu/prices]` change, you text me and it's done. You never log into anything."

## Objection Table

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Too expensive" | "What part feels heavy — the build or the monthly? The build can be phased; the monthly is what keeps it from dying like most sites do." |
| "My nephew/friend can do it" | "Great — the build's the easy half. Who updates it in March when your hours change? That's the part I actually sell." |
| "Facebook works fine" | "Facebook shows you to people who already follow you. Google shows you to people looking for `[niche]` *right now* — that's who a site catches." |
| "I need to think about it" | "Fair. What I'll do is put it all in writing — packages, price, timeline. Nothing to decide on this call. Sound good?" → proposal |
| "Not interested" (hard) | "No problem — thanks for the straight answer. If it changes, you have my number." Log and close. |

## Close

> "Here's the next step: I'll send a one-page proposal by `[tomorrow]` with exactly what we talked about. Look it over, and if it feels right we can have you live by `[date]`. What's the best email?"

## After the Call (same day, no exceptions)

- Log call notes verbatim-ish in the lead file (their words, their priorities)
- Draft the proposal (`templates/proposal.md`) using their language
- Stage → Proposal Sent once delivered; post-call sequence starts (`playbook/sequences.md`)

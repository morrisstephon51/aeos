# Prospecting — Finding Local Businesses That Need a Website

Goal per run: **10 new scored leads** (quota in `config.md`). Personalization data is captured at prospect time — never prospect a name without the details that make the outreach specific.

---

## Where to Look (in order of yield)

### 1. Google Maps sweep
Search `[niche] near [city]` for each niche in `config.md`. For each result check:
- **No website link at all** → highest-value prospect
- **Facebook page instead of a website** → high value
- **Website link that's broken, not mobile-friendly, or visibly ancient** → strong prospect
- Recent reviews but weak web presence = active business, underserved online. Ideal.

### 2. Google Business Profiles with unanswered signals
- Profiles with questions nobody answered, outdated hours, no photos in a year
- These owners are busy, not absent — exactly who buys maintenance

### 3. Facebook/Instagram-only businesses
- Search `[niche] [city]` on Facebook. Active page + no site = they already believe in being online; the upgrade pitch is short.

### 4. New business registrations & "just opened"
- Local news "now open" roundups, chamber of commerce new-member lists, city permit records where public
- New businesses need everything and have no incumbent vendor

### 5. Referral loop (activates after first clients)
- Every launched site gets a "built by Plug AI" footer credit
- Every happy maintenance client gets asked for one introduction at the 90-day mark

## What to Capture Per Prospect

Copy `leads/_TEMPLATE.md` and fill:
- Business name, niche, address/area, phone
- **Website status:** none / social-only / broken / outdated / decent (decent = skip)
- Contact person and channel (owner name from reviews/"about" is gold)
- **One specific observation** — the hook for the first line of outreach ("your menu PDF won't open on a phone", "your top review mentions X")
- Review count + rating (proxy for how alive the business is)

## What NOT to Do

- Don't scrape-and-blast. Ten researched leads beat a hundred names.
- Don't prospect disqualified categories (see `config.md`).
- Don't collect leads without the one specific observation — no hook, no lead.

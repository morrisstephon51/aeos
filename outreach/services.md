# Services — What Plug AI Sells

Two products. Builds get cash in. Maintenance builds the business.

---

## Launch Offer (first 2 clients only — see `config.md`)

**Free Starter build** — paid for by a 6-month maintenance commitment ($100/mo Standard preferred, $50/mo Basic minimum), a testimonial, portfolio rights, and one referral intro at day 60.

- Pitch line: *"I'm launching in Chicago and I want your site in my portfolio — so the build costs you nothing. You only pay the monthly plan that keeps it alive."*
- The 6-month commitment is the exception to our month-to-month rule; it exists because the build is free. Never combine the launch offer with any other discount.
- After client #2 signs, this section is dead. Standard pricing only.

## Website Builds

### Starter — $750–$1,200
- 1–3 pages (home, services, contact)
- Mobile-responsive, click-to-call, Google Maps embed
- Basic SEO (title/meta, Google Business Profile link)
- Delivered in 7 days
- **For:** businesses with no website at all

### Business — $1,500–$2,500
- 4–8 pages, photo gallery, reviews/testimonials section
- Contact forms, booking link integration (Calendly/Square/etc.)
- Local SEO setup, analytics installed
- Delivered in 2–3 weeks
- **For:** businesses replacing an outdated or broken site

### Custom / E-commerce — $3,000+
- Online ordering, payments, menus, inventory, or member portals
- Scoped per project — never quote before the discovery call
- **For:** restaurants wanting online orders, shops wanting a storefront

## Maintenance Plans (attach to EVERY build)

### Basic — $50/mo
- Hosting management, SSL, backups, uptime monitoring
- Up to 1 content change/mo (hours, menu, staff photo)

### Standard — $100/mo
- Everything in Basic
- Up to 4 content changes/mo
- Monthly Google Business Profile refresh
- Quarterly performance report

### Growth — $200/mo
- Everything in Standard
- Unlimited reasonable content changes
- 1 new landing page per quarter
- Local SEO monitoring + review-response drafting

# AXIS — Success Metrics

## What Winning Looks Like

AXIS measures success against one primary question:
> **Is Plug AI a fully operating, revenue-generating legal business entity?**

---

## Milestone Scorecard

| Milestone | Status | Target |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Legal entity formed (LLC/Corp) | ⬜ | Week 2 |
| EIN obtained | ⬜ | Week 2 |
| Business bank account open | ⬜ | Week 3 |
| Business email and domain live | ⬜ | Week 1 |
| First Plug AI event scheduled | ⬜ | Week 4 |
| First paying client/ticket revenue | ⬜ | Week 6 |
| Operations running without daily founder intervention | ⬜ | Week 8 |

---

## Leading Indicators (Weekly Tracking)

- Outreach contacts initiated this week
- Leads in pipeline
- Events scheduled (confirmed vs. tentative)
- Revenue closed ($)
- Open tasks closed vs. created ratio (should trend toward closure)
- Decisions pending from Morris >48h (should be zero)

---

## Lagging Indicators (Monthly)

- Total revenue
- New clients/customers
- Events delivered
- Legal/compliance status (all clean)
- Brand presence (website live, social active)

---

## AXIS Self-Assessment Prompt (Weekly)

Every Monday AXIS asks:
1. What moved the needle on revenue last week?
2. What's the biggest unresolved blocker?
3. Is Morris stuck anywhere? If yes — what's the push?
